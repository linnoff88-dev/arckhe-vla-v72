Lesson 7 · 16 min

Beyond imitation: RL, world models, and memory

Imitation has a ceiling: the policy only learns what the person showed it, and it doesn't know how to recover from a mistake it has never seen. In 2026 three ideas push that ceiling: learning from its own experience (RL), predicting the future in video (World Action Models), and remembering what it has already done (memory).

    What you did in lesson 5: the policy copies the actions in the dataset. It works, but it inherits the limits of the demonstrations.

  2. 2/5

    RL afterward

    After imitating, the policy runs, makes mistakes, and learns from the outcome and from human corrections. According to PI, Recap more than doubled throughput on hard tasks and cut failures by half or more.

  3. 3/5

    World Action Model

    The model predicts the video of what will happen along with the action. The bet is that predicting the future teaches physics better than predicting the action alone.

  4. 4/5

    Memory

    Remember what has already been done so you don't repeat or skip steps. PI's MEM handled tasks longer than 10 minutes, like cleaning an entire kitchen.

  5. 5/5

    The whole frontier

    Imitation gives the start; RL corrects with experience; the World Action Model predicts the future; memory holds long tasks together. The top models of 2026 combine more than one.

02 · The 2026 examples

Click each one. Numbers are as reported by the companies.

1. Recap

RL with corrections and advantage-conditioned policies: the policy learns what went better and what went worse in its own attempts, and from a person's corrections.

According to PI, π*0.6 made espresso from 5:30 a.m. to 11:30 p.m., folded 50 new garments, and assembled 59 boxes in a factory.

2. RL Token

The VLA learns to emit an "RL token" that summarizes what it sees. A small actor-critic trains RL in real time on that token, with just a few hours or minutes of data.

Tested on precision tasks like tightening an M3 screw with an electric screwdriver and plugging in an ethernet cable.

3. DreamZero

A 14B World Action Model that predicts video and action together and controls in a closed loop at 7 Hz. According to the paper, it generalizes more than twice as well as state-of-the-art VLAs on new tasks and environments.

GR00T N2, announced in Mar 2026 on top of this architecture, was expected for late 2026 and had no confirmed release as of Oct 2026.

4. MEM and π0.7

MEM gives the VLA short-term and long-term memory, evaluated on top of π0.6, on tasks longer than 10 minutes.

π0.7 (Apr 2026) accepts quality and speed metadata and subgoal images in the prompt, and learns through language "coaching" to use devices it hasn't seen. It is closed.
