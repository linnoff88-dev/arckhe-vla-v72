Lesson 8 · 18 min

From the lab to the real world

A policy that succeeds in the simulator is still far from a production line. In between are ROS 2, which connects the model to the motors, the safety layers, which no VLA can do without, and the real state of the market, which is less ready than the headlines make it look.

02 · Safety, market, and hardware in 2026

Standards and market come with their source: much of it appeared only in a secondary source.

1. Standards

According to the ISO page seen in a search result, ISO 10218-1 and 10218-2:2025 were published in Feb 2025 and remain in two parts: -1 for the robot and -2 for the application and the cell. According to secondary sources, -2 absorbed the content of ISO/TS 15066 on collaborative robots.

According to Drives & Controls magazine (Oct 18, 2026), ISO 25785-1, for humanoids and other robots with active balance, is still a draft (CD): no manufacturer can claim certification to it.

2. Europe

The European Commission portal (Aug 2026) reports that the Digital Omnibus postponed the AI Act's high-risk rules and removed machinery from the AI Act's own list: the AI requirements for machinery move to the Machinery Regulation.

According to legal analyses (Freshfields, Gibson Dunn), the new deadlines are Dec 2, 2027 (Annex III) and Aug 2, 2028 (Annex I).

3. Market

According to CNBC, Unitree went public in Shanghai at a valuation of about US$ 9 bn (Aug 2026). Also reported were Skild AI's round at more than US$ 14 bn (Jan 2026), Apptronik's (US$ 520 mn, Feb 2026), Agility's merger with a SPAC (Jun 2026), and AgiBot's IPO filing in Hong Kong (Jul 2026).

Physical Intelligence's ~US$ 1 bn round was reported by Bloomberg, with no official announcement. Bank market projections have different scopes (2035 versus 2050, hardware only versus services) and are not comparable with each other.

4. Humanoids

According to press reports, the production Atlas was launched at CES 2026 with the year's batch already spoken for; 1X's NEO had no confirmed customer delivery as of Jul/2026; and Tesla's Optimus V3 had not been shown as of Aug/2026. Most deployments are still pilots or narrow tasks.

Compute: according to CNX Software, the Jetson AGX Thor kit went from US$ 3,499 to US$ 5,499 and the Orin Nano Super from US$ 249 to US$ 399 (Jul/2026).

03 · The checklist for taking a VLA to the real world

Copy it and fill it in for a case of your own. Every line without an answer is a reason not to turn the robot on.

checklist-mundo-real.md
# Checklist: <task> at <place>

## Model
- [ ] Do the weights' license allow this use? (which: ______)
- [ ] Evaluated in simulation: success rate ____ % over ____ episodes
- [ ] Evaluated on the real robot: ____ % over ____ attempts
- [ ] Most common failure noted: ______

## Integration
- [ ] ROS 2 distribution and end-of-support date: ______
- [ ] Policy node publishes to the controller (not straight to the motor)
- [ ] Inference time measured: ____ ms; chunk covers ____ ms

## Safety (outside the model)
- [ ] Emergency stop tested
- [ ] Position, speed, and force limits in the controller
- [ ] Work zone / protection (light curtain, fence, marked area)
- [ ] Applicable standard checked with whoever certifies (ISO 10218-1/-2:2025 or local)
- [ ] What happens if the camera fails or the model hangs: ______

## People
- [ ] Who can take over control and how: ______
- [ ] Log of every intervention (it becomes correction data)
Simulation: illustrative example
  • Task: collaborative arm sorts parts on a lab bench.
  • License: Apache-2.0 (π0.5 from openpi).
  • Sim: 72% over 50 episodes. Real: 55% over 20 attempts; most common failure: shiny part.
  • ROS 2: Lyrical, supported until May/2031.
  • Inference: 180 ms; a 25 to 50 Hz chunk covers 500 ms.
  • Safety: stop tested, force limit in the controller, marked area; standard checked with the integrator.

Numbers made up to show how to fill it in. Yours come from lessons 2, 5, and 6.

  • Putting safety inside the prompt: "don't hit anyone" in the instruction is not protection. Protection is a stop and limits outside the model.
  • Using a ranking as proof: the success rate in your case, measured by you, is worth more than a benchmark score.
  • Treating an announcement as a product: GR00T N2 and Optimus V3, for example, were not available in Oct/2026.
