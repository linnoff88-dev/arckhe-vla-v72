Lesson 1 · 14 min

From LLM to a robot that acts

A chatbot returns text. A robot has to return motion, dozens of times per second, while looking at a camera. This lesson separates the four model families you'll hear about in the course and shows what changed between 2023 and October 2026.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1What goes in and what comes out of each model family
  2. 2Why a VLA returns an action, not text
  3. 3What a World Action Model is, the new category of 2026
  4. 4Classify real systems with the source next to you

Goal: tell LLM, VLM, VLA, and World Action Model apart and say what changed from 2023 to Oct 2026.

01 · The ladder: from text to motion

Each step adds an input or an output. Tap each one.

The question that settles it: what comes out of the model? Text, action, or video + action.

LLMs and VLMs answer. VLAs and WAMs act: the output becomes a motor command.

02 · What changed from 2023 to October 2026

Four milestones. Tap each stage.

1. Data together

Open X-Embodiment (2023) merged dozens of datasets from different labs into a single format. It was the basis for training one model across many robots.

In Jun/2024, the 7B OpenVLA was pretrained on 970 thousand episodes from that collection, with open weights.

The ideaOne model, trained on data from many robots, instead of one model per robot.

2. Continuous action

π0 introduced an "action expert" using flow matching: instead of picking the action from a list of values, it generates the action as a continuous value. The code and weights are in the openpi repository, under the Apache-2.0 license.

Action chunking came along with it and remains a foundation (the OpenVLA-OFT recipe, from Feb/2025, uses it): the model returns a chunk of several actions at once. You will understand both in lesson 2.

3. Whole body

Figure's Helix 02 (Jan/2026) controls the entire humanoid and gained a third layer, S0, running at 1 kHz.

Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics 2 (07/30/2026) controls humanoids "from toes to fingertips" and, according to Google, adapts to a new body usually with fewer than 200 examples.

4. Predicting the world

The World Action Model category emerges: DreamZero (Feb/2026) predicts video and action together. NVIDIA's Cosmos 3 (weights on the Hub on 05/31/2026) can serve as a VLM, a simulator, or a base for this kind of model.

At the same time, PI's frontier models went closed: π0.7 (Apr/2026) has no weights, and on 10/13/2026 PI opened a commercial API in beta.

Why it mattersOpen and closed coexist. In lesson 3 you choose based on what you can actually use.

Simulation: illustrative example
  • OpenVLA: camera + sentence → action. VLA. openvla.github.io
  • π0.5: camera + sentence + state → action. VLA. github.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi
  • Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6: images + question → spatial reasoning, points, instrument reading. Embodied reasoning model (VLM family): it does not move the robot on its own. deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-er-1-6/
  • DreamZero: camera + sentence → future video + action. WAM. arxiv.org/abs/2602.15922
  • Cosmos 3: world foundation model that combines world generation, reasoning, and action prediction; it serves as a base for WAM. huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos3-Super
  • SmolVLA: camera + sentence + state → action. Small VLA (450M). huggingface.co/blog/smolvla
  • Calling every robot model a VLA: ER 1.6 reasons and points, but a different policy moves the arm.
  • Secondhand sources: news helps you find things, but the sheet asks for the official blog, the paper, or the model card.
